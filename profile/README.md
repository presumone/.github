## Two stages, one shared purpose

We connect practical learning with professional collaboration. Members build skills, show evidence of their progress, and grow into contributors who can deliver with a team.

| | PreSum | SumOne |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Learning, practice, and readiness | Professional collaboration and delivery |
| For | Learners and aspiring earners | Verified professionals and project contributors |
| Journey | Learn → Practice → Build → Prove → Earn | Collaborate → Deliver → Lead → Create Opportunities |
| Evidence | Working projects, clear explanations, and consistent progress | Reliable delivery, teamwork, and accountable ownership |

## How members grow

**Learning tracks → Individual projects → Team projects → Evaluation → Real project contribution → Verified professional → SumOne**

1. **Learn and practice:** follow a structured track and apply each concept.
2. **Build and explain:** complete individual projects and demonstrate how they work.
3. **Collaborate:** use shared workflows, respond to feedback, and deliver team projects.
4. **Prove readiness:** present working results and explain your decisions during evaluation.
5. **Contribute and lead:** take responsibility for real work and help others grow.

Progress depends on demonstrated skills and reliability. Learning-track completion does not automatically guarantee paid work or professional verification.

## Ways to contribute

- **Learners:** follow published specifications and submit your own implementation with evidence.
- **Contributors:** improve documentation, reproduce bugs, propose fixes, and review work.
- **Mentors:** explain concepts and offer specific, actionable feedback.
- **Professionals:** collaborate on scoped projects, communicate progress, and own delivery commitments.

Start with the target repository's README and [our contribution guide](https://github.com/presumone/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What we value

- **Skill + reliability:** build competence and follow through on commitments.
- **Freedom + responsibility:** take initiative and communicate the impact of your decisions.
- **Learn by building:** turn understanding into working results.
- **Clear work + clear proof:** make progress, sources, and contributions easy to review.
- **Create opportunities together:** share knowledge, give credit, and support each other's growth.

Follow our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/presumone/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). For sensitive technical reports, read our [security guidance](https://github.com/presumone/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
