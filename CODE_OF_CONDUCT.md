# PreSumOne Code of Conduct

Everyone deserves respectful participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient and respectful, especially when others are learning.
- Give constructive feedback focused on work and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and others' time.
- Be honest about your experience, contributions, and commitments.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, unauthorized sharing of private information, impersonation, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply in PreSumOne repositories, reviews, discussions, events, other community spaces, and when representing PreSumOne publicly.

Contact an organization owner or project maintainer through an established private community channel. Describe what happened and include relevant evidence. Do not post sensitive details publicly. If a concern involves a maintainer, contact another organization owner where possible.

A dedicated private reporting contact has not yet been published in this repository. If you cannot reach a suitable maintainer, use the hosting platform's reporting tools for abusive content.

## Response

Maintainers should review concerns fairly and limit disclosure to people needed to address the report. Depending on context and severity, responses may include requesting a correction, removing content, issuing a warning, or restricting participation. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from community spaces.

Participants may ask an uninvolved organization owner to review a decision where one is available.
